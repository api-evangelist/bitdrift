bitdrift Capture just got even more powerful: as of today, we’ve added app stability insights! Built on top of our crash reporting support we now automatically surface [app stability scores](https://docs.bitdrift.io/product/instant-insights-features#stability). This is calculated as the percentage of users who remained crash-free within a given time period and/or app version.
