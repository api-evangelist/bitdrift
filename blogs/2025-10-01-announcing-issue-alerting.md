bitdrift Capture just got even more powerful: as of today, we've added alerting and notifications for issues and crash reports. This feature is critical for mobile engineering teams looking to stay on top of new or trending issues that directly impact users. This is a critical feature that mobile engineering teams need to make sure they stay on top of any new or trending issues that may be impacting their users.
